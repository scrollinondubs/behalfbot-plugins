Founder: What do you think about the state of software for independent pharmacies?
Carla: Honestly, most of it is terrible. Pharmacists are not tech people.
Founder: Do you think pharmacies would switch to something better?
Carla: They might, if it was cheap. People in my industry are very conservative though.
Founder: What do you think the biggest problem is?
Carla: Probably stock management. Everyone says it's a pain.
Founder: Would you be interested in trying a new tool?
Carla: Maybe. I could maybe introduce you to some people sometime, if it gets good.
