You quote a kitchen renovation, the client says yes, and three weeks later the tile price has gone up 12 percent.

You eat the difference because re-quoting feels unprofessional, and that is exactly the problem our QuoteGuard app was made to stop.

Imagine every quote you send already allowing for price changes, and clients accepting it without a second conversation.

The fix is a price-validity clause: state how long the quote holds, name the materials that can move, and say how you will pass a rise on. Add it to your next quote.
