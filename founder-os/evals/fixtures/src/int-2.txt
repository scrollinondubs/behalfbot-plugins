Founder: When was the last time a client paid you late?
Joana: Last month, actually. A restaurant group owed us 4,200 euros for a branding job and paid 61 days after the invoice date.
Founder: What did you do while you waited?
Joana: I sent five reminder emails by hand and on day 45 I phoned their finance person. I also had to delay paying our illustrator by two weeks.
Founder: Is that normal for you, or was that one unusual?
Joana: It happens three or four times a year. I built a spreadsheet in 2024 that colours every invoice red after 30 days, and I check it every Monday.
Founder: What have you tried besides the spreadsheet?
Joana: We tried the reminder feature in our accounting software in January and switched it off after a week, because it sent the same stiff email to clients we are friendly with.
Founder: Who else in the studio deals with this?
Joana: My business partner Rui handles the finance side. I can introduce you to him this week, he'll have the exact numbers.
