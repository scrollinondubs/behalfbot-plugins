You finish a job, send the invoice, and then nothing happens for six weeks. Every day you check the bank app and every day it is the same number.

Then you have to write the email. Too soft and they ignore it. Too hard and you lose the client who pays most of your rent.

Imagine getting paid on the due date without ever having to write a single awkward sentence, and the client thanking you for the reminder.

Here is the method: put the payment date in the proposal, send a friendly note three days before it is due, and on the due date send a second note that names the amount and the date. Clients pay a reminder they saw coming.
