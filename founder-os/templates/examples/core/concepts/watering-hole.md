# A watering hole is where an audience talks shop

A watering hole is any place where the people you want to serve already talk to
each other about the work you want to help with.

It matters because it is where you hear problems in their own words, before you
have shaped the question. A good one is busy, on topic, and full of members
answering each other rather than broadcasting. It can be a forum, a group chat, a
comment section, or a room at a trade event.

Finding the watering holes comes before building anything. That ordering is its
own idea: [[audience-before-product]].
