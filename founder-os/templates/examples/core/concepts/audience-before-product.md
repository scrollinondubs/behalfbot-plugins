# Choose the audience before the product

Pick the people first, then learn what they need, then build for it. Starting
from a product idea and hunting for buyers usually runs the other way.

A founder who starts from the product hears every conversation as a pitch
opportunity. A founder who starts from the audience hears problems, and can
build something for the loudest one. The first place to listen is their
[[watering-hole|watering holes]].
