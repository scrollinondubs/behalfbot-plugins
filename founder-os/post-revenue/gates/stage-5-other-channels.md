## What this is
You have a funnel that converts, automations that follow up, loops that compound and outreach that works. Now you add channels that reach people you could never reach one by one: partners who sell for you, ads that follow up with past visitors and find people like your customers, and feeder sites and lead magnets that pull in search traffic for years.

## Why you care
Every channel saturates eventually. The founders who keep growing are the ones who add the next channel before the current one flattens out.

## What you get
A partner program with its first partner signed, a retargeting and lookalike campaign with numbers, and a feeder site or advanced lead magnet live and collecting leads.
