## What this is
Cold outreach means contacting people who have never heard of you and starting a real conversation. You run your first campaign by hand, then bring in the tools that find addresses and send email at scale, then learn the techniques Sean used as head of sales at Pagely to make outreach feel personal at volume.

## Why you care
Inbound takes time to build. Outreach works this week. It is also the fastest way to learn how your best customers describe their problem, because you are talking to them one at a time.

## What you get
One campaign run by hand with real replies, the same campaign rebuilt with tools and your agent, and one advanced technique tested on it.
