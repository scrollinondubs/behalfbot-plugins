## What this is
You have customers. Now you learn to see your business as a path people walk: from never having heard of you, to visitor, lead, customer, repeat customer and the person who sends you their friends. You name each step, put a number on it, and draw the funnel you actually run today. Your agent wires up the tracking; you decide what is worth counting.

## Why you care
Everything later in this track is about moving people from one step to the next. You cannot fix a step you cannot see. Most founders at this point are guessing, and the guess is usually wrong about where people drop off.

## What you get
A lifecycle map in your own words, analytics that report real numbers for each step, and a funnel map that shows where people come in and where they leak out.
