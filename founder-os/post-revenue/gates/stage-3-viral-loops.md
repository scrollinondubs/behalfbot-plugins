## What this is
Until now, growth has meant you going out and finding people. This stage builds growth into the product and the customer base itself: an affiliate program that pays your fans, a viral loop where using the product brings in new users, and your Sales Safari and e-bomb habit turned into a system your agent runs.

## Why you care
Paid and manual channels grow in a straight line with your effort and budget. Loops compound. A customer who brings one more customer, who brings one more, is the cheapest growth you will ever get.

## What you get
A working affiliate program, one viral loop designed and shipped, and an automated research and content pipeline that keeps feeding your list.
