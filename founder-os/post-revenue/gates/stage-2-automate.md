## What this is
You understand your lifecycle, you can measure it, you know where the hose is kinked and you have run experiments on it. Now you automate. A lead magnet and a follow-up sequence, a CRM that fits how you actually sell, and a plain-paper design of who does what and what happens when they do not.

## Why you care
Automation multiplies whatever it touches, including a broken process. That is why this stage comes after the funnel is fixed, not before. Done in that order, it is the moment the business starts working while you sleep.

## What you get
A live lead magnet with a follow-up sequence, a CRM you built or connected, and a We do / They do map ready for your agent to turn into automations.
