## What this is
Five things that make everything else go further: a model of your business you can ask what-if questions of, an AI executive assistant that takes work off your plate, a Business Model Canvas you keep current as the business changes, an advisory board, and a clear-eyed look at whether to raise money.

## Why you care
None of these is a channel. Each one multiplies your time, your judgment or your runway. Skip them and you will still grow, just slower and with more of the load on you.

## What you get
A working financial model, a personal agent handling real tasks, a current Business Model Canvas, a plan for your advisory board, and a written decision on fundraising.
