## What this is
Picture a garden hose. At the tap end are 8 billion people. Then the people online, the people you can reach, the people who visited your site, the leads, the customers, the ones who bought again, the ones who referred a friend. Each pool is smaller than the last. Somewhere in that hose there is a kink. This stage is about finding it, then running small, cheap experiments on it.

## Why you care
Should you buy more traffic, improve the conversion rate on your site, or make the product better? This is not a science, it is an art, and founders burn months fixing the wrong end of the hose. Picking the right leak is worth more than any single tactic.

## What you get
A written call on where the biggest leak is and why, a first ad experiment, and a first A/B test, each with a result you can read.
