## What this is
This one comes from Sean's own experience rather than a book. A feeder site is a small, standalone site or tool that solves one problem for your audience, ranks or spreads on its own, and sends the right people to your main business. An advanced lead magnet goes past the PDF: a calculator, a grader, a free tool, a quiz that gives a useful answer.

**The best feeders are useful enough that people would share them even if your business did not exist.** With an agent doing the building, a small tool like this is now a weekend, not a quarter.

## What you make
One feeder site or advanced lead magnet, live, built around a pain from your research, with a clear path into your main funnel. Your agent builds it; you pick the problem and design the handoff.

## How to submit
Share the link and write which pain it solves, how people will find it, how it hands them to your funnel, and the first numbers.

## Done when
- It solves one real problem from your research and works without signing up first.
- There is a clear, optional next step into your funnel (sign-up, lead magnet, trial).
- You know how people will find it: search, a community, a partner, or your own list.
- It is tracked, so you can see visitors and how many move into your funnel.

## Coach checks
- Feeders that are thin ads for the main product fail. It has to stand on its own.
- Gating the useful part behind an email is fine for a lead magnet, but a feeder site should give real value first.
- Encourage reuse of Safari pains rather than inventing a problem.
- Sean's past posts on Grid7 are the place to point for examples.
