## What this is
Partners are other businesses that already have your customers' trust: agencies, consultants, complementary products, communities. Hans Peter Bech's Building Successful Partner Channels lays out how to build that system on purpose: which partner types fit, what each one gets out of it, and how to recruit, enable and grow them.

**A partner sells for you only when selling you makes them look good to their own customers.** Start from what they win, not what you want.

## What you make
A partner program on one page: the partner type you are going after and why, what they get (revenue share, a better offer for their clients, less work), what you give them to sell with, and a short list of target partners. Your agent researches the targets and drafts the outreach; you hold the first conversations.

## How to submit
Write up your partner program, the list of targets, how many you contacted, and where each conversation stands.

## Done when
- You chose one partner type and said why it fits your customers.
- The offer says plainly what the partner gains.
- You contacted at least ten target partners.
- At least one partner agreed to a first referral or a pilot, or you wrote what the conversations taught you and what you will change.

## Coach checks
- "Partner with everyone" fails. One type, chosen on purpose.
- Partners need material to sell with: a one-pager, a demo, a referral link. Ask what they hand over.
- Biz dev cycles are slow. A signed partner in the time frame is a bonus, not the bar; real conversations and lessons pass.
- The affiliate program from stage 4 can often be extended to partners. Point that out if it fits.
