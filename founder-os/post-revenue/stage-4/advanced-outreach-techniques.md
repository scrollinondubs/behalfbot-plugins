## What this is
Sean was Director of Sales at Pagely from 2015 to 2020, and he wrote up what worked. One of the best: adaptive video. Pagely built a video experience that let visitors get the consultative sales conversation they wanted without waiting for a salesperson, and it brought in more leads and fewer bounces from the quote page.

Social engineering here means understanding people: what makes someone feel seen, curious, or safe enough to reply. **The goal is to be the most relevant message in their inbox, never to trick them.** Personal video, a genuinely useful teardown of their site, a mutual connection's introduction, a reply that references something they wrote last week.

## What you make
One advanced technique added to your outreach and tested against your current campaign. Your agent can generate the personalised parts at scale; you set the script and approve what goes out.

## How to submit
Describe the technique, share an example (a link to a video or page if you have one), and compare its numbers with your standard campaign.

## Done when
- The technique makes each message more relevant to the person, not just more elaborate.
- You ran it on a real batch alongside your standard campaign.
- You compared replies or meetings between the two.
- Nothing in it misleads the recipient about who you are or why you are writing.

## Coach checks
- Deception fails outright: fake replies ("Re:" on a first email), pretending to be a customer, invented mutual connections, fake urgency.
- Video and personalised pages are examples, not requirements. Any technique that raises relevance passes.
- A technique that costs ten times the effort for a small lift is worth naming. Help them judge whether it scales.
- Point them to the Pagely post for the full story and results.
