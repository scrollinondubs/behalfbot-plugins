## What this is
Your hand-run campaign worked well enough to repeat. Now you bring in the tools Sean uses. GetProspect and Hunter find and verify work email addresses. Resend sends email from your own domain through an API, so your agent can send, track and follow up.

**Tools make a working campaign bigger.** Point them at a broken one and you just fail faster, which is why this card comes second.

## What you make
The campaign from the last card, rebuilt as a pipeline: a target list found with an email finder and verified, messages your agent drafts from what it learns about each person, and sending through Resend from a properly set up domain. You approve the template and the list; your agent does the rest.

## How to submit
Describe the pipeline: where the list comes from, how addresses are verified, how messages are personalised, how sending and follow-ups work, and the first batch's numbers.

## Done when
- Addresses are verified before sending, and bounces are under control.
- Your sending domain is set up so mail is authenticated and does not land in spam.
- Each message still carries something specific to the person.
- Every email has an easy way to opt out, and opt-outs are honoured automatically.
- You sent a first batch and compared its reply rate with your hand-run campaign.

## Coach checks
- Sending cold email from your main domain risks your everyday email. Suggest a separate sending subdomain.
- A reply rate far below the hand run usually means personalisation was lost. Ask to see three real messages.
- Any equivalent tools pass. These three are Sean's picks, not requirements.
- Opt-out handling is required. Check that it is automatic.
