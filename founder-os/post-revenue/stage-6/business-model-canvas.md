## What this is
Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur's Business Model Generation gave us the Business Model Canvas: nine boxes on one page that describe how a business creates, delivers and captures value. Customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partners and cost structure.

Before revenue, a canvas is guesswork. After revenue, it is a picture of what is actually happening, and **the useful part is watching which box changes as you grow.**

## What you make
A canvas of your business as it is today, filled from what you have learned in this track, and a second version showing the change you are betting on next, with the boxes that differ marked.

## How to submit
Paste both canvases as text, or upload them, and write two or three sentences on what changes and why.

## Done when
- All nine boxes are filled from evidence, such as your funnel, your customers and your numbers.
- The second canvas changes at least one box on purpose and marks it.
- You wrote which box is the riskiest bet and how you will test it.

## Coach checks
- Canvases full of generic words ("quality", "innovation") fail. Ask for the specific customer, channel and price.
- The point is iteration. A single static canvas without the second version is incomplete.
- Key partners should reflect the partner work from stage 6 if they did it.
