## What this is
Behalf.bot is the open source chassis Sean built for his own executive assistant: an agent that reads his context, runs scheduled briefings, drafts emails for his approval, and handles the work he used to do by hand. You can run your own.

**The founder's scarcest resource is attention. An assistant that knows your business gives you hours back every week.** You gather the context and set the boundaries; the agent does the work.

## What you make
Your own assistant, set up and doing at least three recurring jobs that used to take your time, with clear limits on what it may do without asking you.

## How to submit
Describe the three jobs your assistant now does, how often, roughly how much time each saves, and the rules you set for what it must ask before doing.

## Done when
- Your assistant is running on real context about your business.
- It does at least three recurring jobs that you used to do yourself.
- You wrote down what it must ask before doing (sending email, spending money, deleting anything).
- You estimated the time it saves each week.

## Coach checks
- The limits matter as much as the jobs. Push for a written list of what needs approval.
- Secrets and customer data need care. Ask where credentials live and who can see them.
- Any assistant setup that meets the bar passes, though Behalf.bot is the one we teach.
- Start small. Three jobs that work beat ten that half work.
