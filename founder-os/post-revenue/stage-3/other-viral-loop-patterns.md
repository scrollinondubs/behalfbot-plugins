## What this is
A viral loop is virality built into the product itself. Using the product exposes it to someone new, and some of those people start using it too. Think of a shared document that invites a collaborator, a report with "made with" on it, a free tool that is useful on its own, or an invite that gives both sides something.

The affiliate program pays people to spread the word. **A good viral loop makes spreading the word a side effect of using the product.**

## What you make
One viral loop designed for your product and shipped. Map the loop step by step: who sees what, why they act, and how that brings in the next user. Your agent builds the feature; you design the loop and choose the incentive.

## How to submit
Write the loop as numbered steps, say which step you shipped, and give the numbers: how many people saw it, how many acted, and how many new users came in.

## Done when
- The loop is written as steps that close back on themselves, from a user to a new user.
- The new user has a reason to act that is not just a favour to the inviter.
- The loop is live and tracked, with a first count of how many new users it brought in.
- You estimated how many new users each user brings in, even if the number is small.

## Coach checks
- Many products have a weak natural loop. A small, honest loop beats a forced one that annoys users.
- Spammy patterns (auto-importing contacts, inviting without consent) fail.
- The estimate of new users per user does not have to be good, it has to be measured. Help them read it.
- If they need more time to collect numbers, accept a live, tracked loop with a date for the first count.
