## What this is
Much of what Sean knows about growth loops came from Reforge, a program where people who built growth at companies like Uber and Airbnb taught product strategy and growth. Their curriculum has changed since, so we cite Reforge's growth programs in general.

An affiliate program is the easiest loop to start with. Your users become your advocates, and you pay them a commission for the customers they send. **Your happiest customers are already telling people about you. An affiliate program just says thank you in cash.**

## What you make
A live affiliate program: the commission and terms, a way for affiliates to sign up and get a tracked link, tracking from that link through to a sale, and a short invite to the customers most likely to join.

## How to submit
Share the link where affiliates sign up, and write the commission, the cookie or attribution window, how payouts work, and how many people you invited.

## Done when
- The commission is set from your numbers: you know what a customer is worth and what you can afford to pay.
- A test purchase through an affiliate link was tracked to the right affiliate.
- The terms say how and when affiliates get paid, and what is not allowed (for example, bidding on your brand name).
- You invited at least five real customers or fans by name.

## Coach checks
- A commission picked by feel fails. Ask for the margin and the lifetime value behind the number.
- Attribution is the part that breaks. The test purchase is required.
- Payout terms protect the founder from fraud and refunds. Check for a holding period.
- Five named invites is the bar, not a public launch. Fans first.
- Any tool or a vibecoded tracker passes.
