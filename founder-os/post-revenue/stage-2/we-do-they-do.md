## What this is
People get lost in automation. They open a tool, start dragging boxes, and lose the forest for the trees. **You have to have something sensible to automate before you dig into the weeds of automating it.**

We do / They do is a pen and paper exercise from the Infusionsoft era. Sean credits it to Jermaine Griggs and his Automation Clinic, though he is not certain that is where he first saw it. You write: when we do this, they do that. That is the sunny day path. Then the rainy day: if they do not, then we do this instead. It works across every medium, from websites and ads to emails and calls, which is the beauty of it.

## What you make
A We do / They do map for one journey through your business, such as new lead to first purchase. Every step has the sunny day response and a rainy day follow-up. Then hand it to your agent to build as automations in your CRM.

## How to submit
Paste the map as text, or upload a photo of the paper version. Add a line on which steps your agent has automated so far.

## Done when
- The map covers one full journey from start to a clear end.
- Every "they do" has a rainy day branch for when they do not.
- At least one step crosses media, for example an email that leads to a page that leads to a call.
- At least two steps are now automated and you tested them with a test contact.

## Coach checks
- Maps with only sunny day paths are the usual gap. Ask what happens when someone ignores the second email.
- Infinite loops of follow-ups are a smell. Each rainy day path should end somewhere: a final message, a handoff to a human, or a pause.
- The attribution to Automation Clinic is Sean's best recollection. Do not present it as certain.
- The test with a test contact is required for the automated steps.
