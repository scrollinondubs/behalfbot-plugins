## What this is
You could connect HubSpot, ActiveCampaign or Salesforce and bend your process to fit them. That used to be the only sensible option. **It is now just as easy to vibecode the CRM you actually want**, with exactly the fields, stages and views your business needs and nothing else.

Our Build School course, Getting Started with Claude Code, is the way in if you have not built something like this before. Either path is fine. What matters is that every lead and customer lives in one place, with their stage and history, so the automations in the next card have something to act on.

## What you make
A CRM that holds your leads and customers, shows which lifecycle phase each is in, and records what happened with them. Your agent builds it or connects the tool; you design the stages and decide what a person needs to do to move between them.

## How to submit
Choose which path you took, share a link or screenshot link, and list the stages and the rule for moving between each.

## Done when
- Your real leads and customers are in it, not sample data.
- Each person has a stage that matches your lifecycle map.
- You wrote the rule for moving between each pair of stages.
- New sign-ups from your lead magnet arrive in it on their own.

## Coach checks
- Over-building is the risk on the vibecode path. A list with stages and notes is enough to pass. Dashboards can come later.
- On the integrate path, check that the stages were adapted to their lifecycle rather than left as the vendor defaults.
- The automatic arrival of new leads is the real test. Ask them to show a lead that came in without manual entry.
- Personal data deserves care: ask where the data lives and who can see it.
