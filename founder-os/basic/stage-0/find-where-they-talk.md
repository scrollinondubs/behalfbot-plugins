## What this is
The places your audience gathers online are what the 30x500 method calls watering holes: forums, groups, subreddits, mailing lists. People write things there late at night that they would never say to your face, so this is where you listen. Being a member of some of them already is fine, even helpful: you know where the good threads are. You find them by pairing what the audience calls itself, the tools and jargon it uses, and venue words like forum or group. If nothing turns up, look for your group inside a bigger community.

## What you make
A list of three or more watering holes, each with a link, what sort of place it is, and one line on why it looks worth mining.

## How to submit
Write your list with links in the box, and add the search words you used.

## Done when
- You list three or more watering holes, each with a working link.
- Your search words are included: names the audience calls itself and venue and/or jargon & tool words.
- At least three of your places have threads where members talk with each other, with enough posts and comments to mine for pain and jargon. Old or quiet places count.

## Coach checks
- Did  they just pick the first 3 watering holes to complete the exercise or did they read through the options and choose a more optimal mix based on what they read? 
- A mix of formats is a good sign: forums, groups, product support communities, review threads, marketplaces.
- Did they include some detail on what their watering hole criteria are and why they chose these specific ones? 
- Common mistake: declaring the audience offline before searching inside bigger communities.
- Judge each place on fit: is this audience there, do members talk with each other, does pain show up, is it on topic. Recent activity is not a gate: a quiet place with deep archives is still good Safari ground. Only rule a place out for something you can see, like all vendor posts or not the intended audience or paltry volume of posts.
- If they listed more than 10 places, accept the card and help them narrow down to three most promising to start their Safari with, then suggest how to widen the list later.
- Places they already belong to, or have posted in, count the same as new ones. Painstorming comes later in stage 1, so do not ask for it here.
