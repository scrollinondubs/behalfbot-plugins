## What this is
The first e-bomb is the easy one. Most people stop after it, because the novelty wears off before the habit forms. Write four more, each answering a different question from your Safari notes, and share each one the way you shared the first: a helpful comment in a watering hole and an email to your list.

Use these four to get better at sharing. Robert Cialdini found seven shortcuts people use when they decide to say yes. Each one works when it is true and backfires when it is faked:

- Reciprocity: people give back to whoever gave first. Your e-bomb is the gift, so give the whole answer.
- Consistency: people stick with what they already said or did. Ask for one small step, like replying with their own question.
- Social proof: people look at what others like them do. Mention real replies and results from readers, never made-up ones.
- Authority: people follow someone who knows the subject. Show it through your work and your own experience, not claims about yourself.
- Liking: people say yes to people they like. Use their words, name what you have in common, and thank the people who help you.
- Scarcity: people want what they might miss. Use it only when it is real, like a live session on a set date. Never invent a deadline.
- Unity: people say yes to "one of us". You chose an audience you belong to, so write as one of them.

Every e-bomb already uses reciprocity. Pick one more principle for each new e-bomb and use it on purpose in the comment and the email.

## What you make
Four more published e-bombs, five with your first, and proof that you shared each one in a watering hole and sent it to your list.

## How to submit
Share the link to each new e-bomb and the link to the watering hole comment where you shared it. For each email to your list, share a link to it or upload a screenshot.

## Done when
- Four more e-bombs are live on your site, five in all with your first.
- Each new e-bomb answers a different question from your Safari notes.
- Each new e-bomb was shared in at least one watering hole and sent to your list.
- Each share and each email has a link or a screenshot as proof.

## Coach checks
- Same recipe as the first e-bomb: one real question, proof they understand the situation, one answer, the call to action. Its coach checks apply here too.
- Four different questions, not one question rewritten four ways.
- Shares still pass the living room test. A comment that is mostly the link fails (pp 101, 109-110).
- Honest use only. A made-up testimonial, an invented reader count, a fake deadline or a fake "only 3 spots left" fails the card, even if it worked.
- Cialdini's page explains six principles in detail and marks unity, the seventh, with an asterisk because it was added later. The card teaches all seven.
- Common mistake: tweaking headlines and tweets instead of writing the next e-bomb (p 107).
- Four more is a FounderOS number. The course says people need many exposures before they buy (p 62).
