## What this is
You cannot read the whole internet, so survey one watering hole from the top. Read only the topic titles on the first two pages, collect the words and questions people keep using, and group them into themes in whatever way makes sense to you. Then search the place for those words and save every thread that looks even a little promising, without reading it yet.

## What you make
A word list from one watering hole's topic titles, grouped into named themes, plus ten or more saved threads you found with those words.

## How to submit
Upload your notes or share a link to them, with your saved thread links listed.

## Done when
- You took your words from topic titles only, across at least the first two pages of one watering hole.
- Your words are grouped into named themes, each with a line on what it suggests about the audience.
- You saved ten or more threads found by searching the watering hole for those words.
- Your saved threads include at least one with many replies and one with few or none.
- You searched at least one more watering hole with the same words.

## Coach checks
- Are the themes about the audience's problems and questions, or about the forum's housekeeping (pp 73-74)?
- Did they group the words their own way, or force them into someone else's categories (p 76)?
- A pattern across many posters is what shows a pain belongs to the audience and not to one person (pp 76-77). These themes become e-bomb topics later.
- Did they judge threads before saving them instead of grabbing anything promising (p 82)?
- An unanswered question can itself be a pain (p 82).
- Common mistake: spending the session choosing a bookmarking tool (p 82).
- Ten threads is a FounderOS number. One search word found the course's demo twenty-plus (p 81).
