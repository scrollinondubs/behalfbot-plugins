## What this is
You pick one group of people to study, find the places online where they talk, and get clear on why you are doing this. It takes a few hours, not weeks.

## Why you care
You cannot build something people already want until you know who those people are. This is not a lifetime choice. You are picking one group to research, and the research will tell you whether it was a good pick.

## What you get
A named audience you already belong to, three or more lively places online where they talk, and a clear picture of why this work matters to you.
