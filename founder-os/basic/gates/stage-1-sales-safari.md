## What this is
Sales Safari is how you learn what your audience needs, wants and already buys, by reading what they write to each other. No surveys and no guessing. You read one thread closely, zoom out to the themes, then collect the details that make your writing feel personal.

## Why you care
Safari is the engine of 30x500. Almost everything you do later runs on what you find here. It feels hard for the first few dozen threads, and that is normal.

## What you get
Notes full of your audience's own words, sorted into pain, jargon, worldview and what they buy, a list of themes that keep coming up, and your first five e-bomb ideas.
