# A watering hole is where an audience already talks shop

A watering hole is any place where the people you want to serve already talk to
each other about the work you want to help with.

It matters because it is where you hear problems in their own words, before you
have shaped the question. A good one is busy, on topic, and full of members
answering each other rather than a vendor answering them. It can be a forum, a
group chat, a subreddit, a comment section or a room at a trade event. A
product's own help forum usually fails the test: the talk there is about the
product, not the job.

Finding them comes before building anything, and reading them is how a founder
earns the right to sell later.
