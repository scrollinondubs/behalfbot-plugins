# A foothold is a segment incumbents will not fight for

A foothold is the first market a new product wins because the established
players either cannot serve it or do not want to.

There are two usual kinds. Low-end: customers who are overserved and overcharged
by the incumbent and would happily take something simpler and cheaper, and the
incumbent is glad to let them go because they are its least profitable
customers. New-market: people who do not use any product for the job today,
because the existing ones are too expensive or too complicated, and who
currently get by with workarounds. Either way the test is the same: would an
incumbent defend this segment? If yes, it is not a foothold.
