# Ask about what people did, not what they think they would do

The only reliable data in a customer conversation is what the person has
already done: when it last happened, what it cost them, what they tried.

Opinions, predictions and compliments are free to give and say more about the
person's politeness than about their problem. "Would you use this?" invites a
kind lie. "When did this last happen, and what did you do about it?" invites a
story with a date in it. A founder who pitches early turns every answer into an
opinion about the pitch. Count the dated incidents in an interview, strike the
compliments, and flag every "would", "probably" and "everyone" as fluff.
