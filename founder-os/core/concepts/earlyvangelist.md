# An earlyvangelist has the problem, knows it, and is already trying to fix it

An earlyvangelist is a customer who will take a chance on an unfinished product
because the problem already costs them enough that they are hunting for a way
out.

The word is Steve Blank's, and so are the criteria behind it: the person has the
problem, knows they have it, is actively looking for a fix, has already patched
together a workaround, and has or can find money to pay. The more of those a
person shows in what they have done, not what they say they would do, the more
likely they are to buy early and forgive rough edges. Someone who is merely
enthusiastic in a call is not one. Look for the spreadsheet they built or the
tool they already pay for, and treat their willingness to take a next step as
the test: a [[commitment-signal]].

Credit: Steve Blank, The Four Steps to the Epiphany and The Startup Owner's
Manual (with Bob Dorf). Read the original: https://steveblank.com/books-for-startups/
